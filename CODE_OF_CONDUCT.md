# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors should:

- Communicate respectfully
- Provide constructive feedback
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences
- Focus on improving the project and community

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to violations, including removing comments, closing discussions, or restricting participation.

## Reporting

For sensitive concerns, please contact the project maintainers through the private reporting channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
